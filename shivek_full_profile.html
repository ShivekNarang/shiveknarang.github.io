Research Experience

  • NeuroRestore, EPFL/CHUV — Fulbright Research Fellow
    Aug 2026 – Jul 2027
    Closed-loop adaptive deep brain stimulation for Parkinson’s disease under Professors Courtine and Bloch. Machine learning biomarker identification and wearable sensor monitoring for gait restoration. Professors Courtine and Bloch received the Queen Elisabeth Prize for Engineering 2026.
    neurorestore.swiss →
  • rNET Lab, Stanford University — Lead Analyst
    Sep 2025 – Present
    Intracranial sEEG dataset from 24 patients with drug-resistant epilepsy. Developed time-resolved wPLV graph models across four frequency bands; alpha band global efficiency and modularity distinguish learners from non-learners. Extended to nodal strength and communicability across Yeo-7 networks. First-author manuscript in preparation.
    Buch Lab, Stanford →
  • Penn Center for Neuroengineering & Therapeutics — Researcher
    2023 – 2026
    Lesion-network connectivity mapping and ALFF analysis in the TRACK-TBI cohort. Finding: limbic network disruption predicts post-traumatic epilepsy risk with greater accuracy than lesion anatomy alone. Conducted under NIH/NINDS K23NS135101. AAN oral presentation 2024. Sigma Xi IFoRE Award 2024. First-author manuscript under review.
    AAN 2024 Coverage →
  • Aravind Eye Hospital, Madurai — CASI Research Fellow
    Summer 2024
    Prospective adherence study across 1,200 patients in neuro-ophthalmology. Finding: limited patient understanding of their condition was the strongest predictor of non-return, regardless of distance or cost. Redesigned follow-up framework with clinical leadership. Co-authored with Dr. Akkayasamy; presented NCNO 2025, PGIMER.
    CASI 2024 Award →
  • Halpern Lab, Penn Functional Neurosurgery — Clinical Researcher
    2021 – 2023

  • Wintermark Neuroradiology Lab, Stanford
