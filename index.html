Shivek Narang

Shivek Narang is a Fulbright U.S. Student Fellow and network neuroscientist. He holds an MSE in Bioengineering and a BA in Neuroscience with Distinction and Cognitive Science, Magna Cum Laude, from the University of Pennsylvania. His research uses lesion-network connectivity mapping, resting-state fMRI, and intracranial sEEG graph models to develop biomarkers of post-traumatic epilepsy risk and cognitive recovery trajectory after TBI. He is the founder of Empower Through Recovery, a 501(c)(3) at the Synaptogenesis Foundation serving acquired brain injury survivors through peer mentorship, vocational reintegration, and longitudinal wellbeing tracking.

Current & Recent Work

  • NeuroRestore, EPFL/CHUV — Fulbright Research Fellow
    Aug 2026 – Jul 2027
    Closed-loop adaptive deep brain stimulation for Parkinson's disease under Professor Grégoire Courtine and Professor Jocelyne Bloch. Partnership with Medtronic. Sensing-enabled DBS devices, machine learning biomarker identification, wearable sensor monitoring for gait restoration. Professors Courtine and Bloch received the Queen Elisabeth Prize for Engineering 2026.
    neurorestore.swiss →
  • rNET Lab, Stanford — Lead Analyst, sEEG Network Dynamics
    Sep 2025 – Present
    Large-scale intracranial sEEG dataset, 24 patients with drug-resistant epilepsy. Time-resolved phase-locking value graph models across four frequency bands. Finding: alpha band global efficiency and modularity distinguish learners from non-learners after Bonferroni correction. First-author manuscript in preparation under Dr. Vivek Buch.
    Buch Lab, Stanford →
  • Penn Center for Neuroengineering & Therapeutics — Researcher
    2023 – 2026
    Lesion-network connectivity mapping in the TRACK-TBI cohort. Limbic network disruption outperforms lesion anatomy as a predictor of post-traumatic epilepsy. AAN Annual Meeting oral presentation, Denver 2024. First-author manuscript submitted to NeuroImage: Clinical. NIH/NINDS K23NS135101. Sigma Xi IFoRE Best Undergraduate Engineering Research Award 2024.
    AAN 2024 Coverage →
  • Empower Through Recovery — Founder & Executive Director
    2023 – Present
    501(c)(3) nonprofit supporting acquired brain injury survivors navigating the post-acute period after clinical rehabilitation ends. 35+ survivors served through Stanford Synapse and Santa Clara Valley Medical Center. Global Neurorecovery Dialogue Series: published conversations with Dr. David Fischer (Penn RECOVER Program), Dr. Matthew Jaffa, and Candace Gantt. Penn President's Engagement Prize Finalist 2025.
    empowerthroughrecovery.org →
  • Aravind Eye Hospital, Madurai — CASI Research Fellow
    Summer 2024
    694 hours. Follow-up adherence study across 1,200 patients in neuro-ophthalmology. Finding: limited patient understanding of their own condition was the strongest independent predictor of non-return, regardless of distance or cost. Redesigned follow-up monitoring framework now in use. Follow-up study underway with Dr. Mahesh Kumar.
    CASI 2024 Award →
  • Halpern Lab, Penn Functional Neurosurgery
    2021 – 2023
    DBS clinic and operating room: pre/post-procedural motor assessments, electrode programming sessions. EEG preprocessing pipelines for neural biomarker research on reward processing and movement. Contributed to work leading to first-in-human closed-loop DBS for OCD (Nho et al., Neuron 2023). Co-authored JMIR Research Protocols 2022.
    JMIR 2022 →
  • Wintermark Neuroradiology Lab, Stanford
    2017 – 2021
    Four years beginning at age fourteen. MR-guided focused ultrasound for drug-resistant temporal lobe epilepsy. Significant reductions in seizure activity in intermediate hippocampus animals. Published Experimental Neurology 2021. MRgFUS now in Phase II clinical trials for drug-resistant epilepsy.
    Experimental Neurology 2021 →
